What is social health

It is not how many friends you have

Social health is how good your relationships actually feel. You can have 500 followers and still feel completely alone. Melo measures the part nobody else does.

How connected you feel

Not how many people you spoke to but whether those conversations actually meant something to you

Whether people drain or energise you

Some relationships leave you feeling full. Others leave you exhausted. Social health tracks the difference

Your patterns over time
