Questions

Good to know

Is Melo free? +

Yes. Melo will be free when it launches. Early access members will always get the core features for free.

When does it launch? +

We are building right now. Waitlist members will be first to get access and will help shape what Melo becomes.

What data does it collect? +

Only what you choose to share. Melo is built on your self-reported check-ins. No phone scanning, no contact access, no passive tracking without your knowledge.

Is this a mental health app? +

Not exactly. Melo is a wellness tracker, like Oura for your sleep. It is not therapy and does not replace professional support. It helps you understand yourself better.

Will my data ever be shared? +

Never. Your data is private and will never be sold or shared with third parties. Full stop.
